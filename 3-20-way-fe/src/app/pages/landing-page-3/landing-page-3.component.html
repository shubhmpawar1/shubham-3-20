How it works

The 3-20 Way app is your gateway to a tailored daily exercise plan — short, easy-to-follow 3 minute videos presented by Karen Case and her team (no fake AI, guaranteed!). Your daily plan runs from as little as 3 minutes to 20 minutes or more, either all at once or spread throughout your day.

A short onboarding questionnaire (just 2–3 minutes) trains the app on your specific health and wellbeing, creating the perfect movement video playlist for you. As your condition or health changes, so does the app.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 3-20 Way?

The 3-20 Way breaks movement into 3 minute sessions. You can do as many sessions as you prefer, ranging from 3 to 20 minutes or more, and spread them across your day. These small moments add up to big changes!

How is this different from free videos online?

Unlike generic online videos, The 3-20 Way creates a personalised daily plan tailored specifically to your health, ability and goals — and it adapts as you progress.

What type of movements are included?

Gentle mobility, strength and balance exercises designed for older adults. Sessions can be done seated, standing or lying down — no equipment required.

How much time does it take?

As little as 3 minutes per day. Your plan is tailored to you and can be up to 20 minutes or more — all spread throughout your day if you prefer.

Who is it for?

The 3-20 Way is designed for older adults and anyone with limited mobility who wants to move more, feel stronger and maintain their independence.

Is it safe?

Yes. All routines are designed by specialist movement coaches with older adults and people with limited mobility in mind — always gentle and always safe.

Do I need equipment?

No equipment or special clothing is needed. All you need is yourself and a safe space to move.

How much does it cost?

£11.99/month or £8.99/month on the annual plan. After your free 10 day trial, you choose the option that suits you best.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, absolutely. Cancel at any time with no hassle and no questions asked.

Can I pay for a gift membership?

Yes! Gift memberships are available for parents, older relatives or friends. A wonderful way to help someone you love move better and feel better.
