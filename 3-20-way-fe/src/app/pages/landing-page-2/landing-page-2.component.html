How it works

The 3-20 Way app is your gateway to a tailored daily exercise plan comprising short, easy to follow 3 minute videos, presented by Karen Case and her team (no fake AI, guaranteed!). Based on what you tell us, your daily plan can be from as little as 3 minutes to 20 minutes or more, which you can either follow along with all at one go, or spread out throughout your day.

A short and simple onboarding questionnaire (takes no more than two to three minutes) trains The 3-20 Way app on your specific health and wellbeing, allowing it to create the perfect movement moment video 'playlist' for you.

As your condition or health changes, so does the app, allowing us to ensure that your plan is perfectly matched to you at all times.
